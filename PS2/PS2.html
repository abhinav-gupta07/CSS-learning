Lata Mangeshkar

Lata Deenanath Mangeshkar (28 September 1929 - 6 February 2022) was one of India's most popular and respected singers, whose career spanned six decades and was filled with achievements. Although Lata sang film and non-film songs in over thirty languages, she is best known as a playback singer in Indian cinema . Along with her sister Asha Bhosle, Lata has made significant contributions to film singing. Lata ji's magical voice is loved throughout the Indian subcontinent and the world. Time magazine has recognized her as the indispensable and undisputed empress of Indian playback singing . The Government of India honored her with the Bharat Ratna . She died of COVID -related complications on 6 February 2022, Sunday, Magh Shukla Paksha Panchami Tithi, VS 2078 (Panchak), at Breach Candy Hospital in Mumbai . She had been ill for some time. Her legendary singing and melodious voice have admirers all over the world. [ 3 ] She is fondly called "Lata Didi." In 2001, she was awarded India's highest civilian honour, the Bharat Ratna.
